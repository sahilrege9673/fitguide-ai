The World Health Organization (WHO) provides global evidence-based public health recommendations for children, adolescents, adults, and older adults regarding the frequency, intensity, and duration of physical activity necessary for prevention of non-communicable diseases and preservation of metabolic, cardiovascular, and mental health.

### Global Recommendations for Adults (Aged 18–64 Years)
1. **Aerobic Activity Thresholds**:
   - All adults should undertake at least 150–300 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic physical activity, or at least 75–150 minutes of vigorous-intensity aerobic physical activity, or an equivalent combination of moderate- and vigorous-intensity activity throughout the week for substantial health benefits.
   - For additional health benefits, including enhanced cardiorespiratory fitness and reduced risk of premature all-cause mortality, adults may increase their moderate-intensity aerobic physical activity beyond 300 minutes per week.

2. **Muscle-Strengthening Activity**:
   - Adults should also engage in muscle-strengthening activities at moderate or greater intensity that involve all major muscle groups on 2 or more days a week, as these provide additional health and metabolic benefits.

3. **Sedentary Behaviour Mitigation**:
   - Adults should limit the amount of time spent being sedentary. Replacing sedentary time with physical activity of any intensity (including light intensity) provides positive health benefits. High levels of sedentary behaviour are directly correlated with elevated cardiovascular disease, type 2 diabetes, and cancer mortality.

### Global Recommendations for Children and Adolescents (Aged 5–17 Years)
- Children and adolescents should do at least an average of 60 minutes per day of moderate-to-vigorous intensity, mostly aerobic, physical activity across the week.
- Vigorous-intensity aerobic activities, as well as those that strengthen muscle and bone, should be incorporated at least 3 days a week.
- Sedentary screen time should be limited, and recreational physical activity should emphasize enjoyment, play, motor skill variety, and social interaction.
